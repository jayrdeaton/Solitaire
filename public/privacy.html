Privacy Policy

Tastic Games. Last updated October 14, 2026.

This policy covers the Tastic games made by Infinite Token: SnakeTastic, HangmanTastic, SolitaireTastic, and LightCyclesTastic, including their iPhone, iPad, Android, and web versions.

The short version: the games don't collect, share, or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Data That Stays on Your Device

Everything a game saves is stored on your device and never sent to us. That includes:

  • Game progress, settings, stats, and achievements
  • Player profiles: the names and colors you choose
  • Puzzle packs you create in HangmanTastic

On iPhone and iPad, player profiles are shared between the Tastic games installed on the same device, using an App Group (an iOS feature that lets apps from the same developer share storage on that device). Profiles never leave your device.

Deleting a game deletes its data. Shared profiles are removed once every Tastic game is deleted from the device. On the web, data is kept in your browser's storage for that site, and clearing your browser data for the site removes it.

Game Updates

When you open one of the mobile apps, it checks for a game update using Expo's update service. That request includes technical details needed to deliver the right update, such as the app version and platform, and a random installation ID that isn't linked to you. Like any internet request, it also includes your IP address. It never includes your profiles, progress, or other game data. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

Web Versions

The web versions are hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard request logs (such as IP addresses) to serve and protect the site. The games don't set cookies. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

Files You Share

HangmanTastic can export puzzle packs and progress backups as files. A file is only created when you ask for one, and it goes only where you send it through your device's share sheet.

Children

The games are made for players of all ages. Because they don't collect personal information from anyone, they don't collect it from children either.
